About Us

Preparing youth for an AI-driven future

Lumina AI School exists because the future our students are walking into won't reward people who only know how to use AI — it will reward the people who know how to build with it. Every programme is designed around the same idea: a student should leave every session having built, coded, or tested something real.

We were officially launched with a ribbon-cutting ceremony and an MOU signing, officiated by the Minister of Science, Technology and Innovation (MOSTI) — recognition of what hands-on AI and robotics education can do for Malaysia's next generation.
